# 3 Sample Posts — Property Renovation Business (Demo)

*Demonstration samples in my style, for a fictional UK property renovation company. The same formats swap easily to restaurants, online shops and coaches.*

## Sample 1 — Reel script (20–30 seconds)
- **Hook (0–3s, on screen):** "This kitchen took 9 days. Watch the last wall come down."
- **Middle:** fast cuts — old units out, tiling, new units in; voiceover/text: "Full strip-out, rewire, plaster, fit. One team, start to finish."
- **End (CTA):** "Planning a renovation this year? DM 'QUOTE' for a free estimate."
- **Caption:** From tired to timeless in 9 days ✨ Full kitchen renovation, done by one team — no chasing five different trades. DM QUOTE for your free estimate. #KitchenRenovation #HomeImprovement #UKHomes #PropertyRenovation #BeforeAndAfter

## Sample 2 — Carousel (6 slides)
1. "5 questions to ask BEFORE you hire a builder"
2. Are they insured — and can they show the certificate?
3. Will you get a written, itemised quote?
4. Who is on site every day — one team or strangers?
5. What happens if the job runs over time?
6. Save this post — and DM us for our free checklist.

## Sample 3 — Trust post + 3 Stories
- **Post caption:** "We don't ask for full payment upfront. You pay in stages, as the work is done and checked by you. That's how it should be." (photo: team on site, work in progress)
